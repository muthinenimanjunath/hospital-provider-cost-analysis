Project Evolution

Phase 1: Descriptive Analytics
Capstone 1

The foundation of this architecture established a baseline understanding of hospital financial health. We consolidated 12 years of CMS data and engineered critical KPIs. We deployed an interactive Power BI Dashboard mapping operational trends and geographic cost disparities.

Key Technologies: Pandas, Seaborn, Power BI
Phase 2: Predictive Intelligence
Capstone 2 (Current)

Transitioning from descriptive to predictive analysis. This Flask application deploys advanced Machine Learning ensembles, allowing stakeholders to input specific hospital metrics to predict profitability and algorithmically segment facilities in real-time.

Key Technologies: Scikit-Learn, Flask, Bootstrap 5

Supervised Learning

1. Profit Margin Prediction
Random Forest Regressor

Trained using a 300-tree ensemble to predict overall profitability.

Test R² Score: 0.4060
2. Charity Care Ratio Prediction
Extra Trees Regressor

Utilizes randomized split points to reduce variance when predicting the ratio of uncompensated care.

Test R² Score: 0.5947

Unsupervised Segmentation

K-Means Clustering

Facilities are segmented into distinct operational archetypes based on a 13-feature standardized matrix.

Identified Archetypes:
High Performing High Utilization Cost Inefficient Underperforming Charity Focused Staff Heavy
